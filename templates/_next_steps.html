What to do with this

These results are a starting point for a conversation, not a finding about you.

If you are worried about a medicine you take, speak to your prescriber or pharmacist. Do not stop taking a prescribed medicine because of anything on this page — stopping can be more dangerous than continuing.

If you believe you have experienced a side effect, you can report it to the FDA directly through MedWatch. Reports like these are what this database is built from, so a report you file becomes part of what a page like this can show.
