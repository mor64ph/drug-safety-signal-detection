What this list is

These are reactions that appear in FAERS reports alongside your drugs far more often than the rest of the database would predict. That is a statement about what gets reported, not about what your medicine does to you, and not a safety assessment of your treatment. Reporting is shaped by how new a drug is, by news coverage and by litigation.

Most of these reactions will never happen to you, and this page cannot tell you how likely any of them is, because the data contains no record of the people who took the drug and were fine. Do not stop or change a medicine because of anything here. Take it to your prescriber instead.
