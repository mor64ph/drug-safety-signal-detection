Create an account

An account lets you keep a list of the drugs you take and be told when the reporting pattern for one of them changes. Searching needs no account at all.

What this is not

reportscope describes how often a reaction is reported alongside a drug in voluntary, unvalidated case reports. It does not measure risk, incidence or causation, it is not a safety assessment of your medicines, and it is not medical advice. Nothing here should change how you take a medicine. That decision belongs with your prescriber.
