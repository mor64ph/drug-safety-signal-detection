Reading a difference

A higher number on one side does not mean that drug is more dangerous. The two drugs are different ages, are prescribed to different people for different lengths of time, and carry different amounts of news coverage and litigation — all of which move these figures without anything pharmacological changing. A reaction scored for only one drug usually means the other had too few reports to score, not that it had none.
