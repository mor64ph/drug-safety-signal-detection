Reading the overlap

Computed over every strong-tier reaction each drug has, not only the {{ per_drug }} shown in its panel above. The figure in each cell is a — how many reports name that drug and that reaction together — with the IC025 beneath it.

Overlap is the normal state of this data, not a finding. {% if widespread | length > 1 %} {{ widespread[0].term }} is reported with {{ widespread[0].drugs }} of the {{ total_drugs }} drugs here and {{ widespread[1].term }} with {{ widespread[1].drugs }}, so terms overlap for any list of drugs anyone enters — the gravest ones included. {% endif %} A reaction shared by several drugs more often reflects that they are prescribed to similar people, for similar lengths of time, at similar ages, than anything the molecules have in common.

A cell marked not strong is not a statement in that drug's favour: the reaction may be moderate or weak for it, or may have had too few reports to score at all.
