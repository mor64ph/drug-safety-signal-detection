A change here is a change in reporting

It means a reaction is now being reported alongside the drug more often than the rest of the database would predict, or more strongly than at the last check — not that the drug has become more dangerous, and not that anything new has been discovered about it. New drugs, media coverage and legal advertising all move these figures without anything changing in the medicine. Bring it to your prescriber rather than acting on it.
