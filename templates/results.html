How to read this

strong IC025 > 2   moderate IC025 > 1   weak IC025 > 0. a is the number of reports naming both the drug and the reaction — always read it alongside the ratio, because a ratio of 300 built on 9 reports is far less informative than a ratio of 13 built on 8,820. ROR compares how often this reaction appears in reports about this drug against every other drug. IC025 is the conservative lower bound used for ranking; it deliberately penalises small counts. None of these columns measures how likely the drug is to cause the reaction, and none can, because the data contains no information about how many people took the drug without incident.

Bias diagnostics are run on the highest-ranked reactions only, because each one costs additional queries. Rows marked not assessed have not been checked for the problems described below — that is not the same as having been checked and cleared.

serious event marks a reaction examined on every drug because of its severity, rather than because it was reported often. Candidates otherwise arrive in order of frequency, which buries rare but grave reactions underneath common complaints: without this sweep, tendon rupture never appeared for fluoroquinolones and Stevens–Johnson syndrome never appeared for lamotrigine, though both are boxed warnings.

Bias flags detail — {{ biased_pairs | length }} pairs flagged
Notoriety spike
More than a quarter of all reports for this pair arrived in one month. Genuine pharmacology accrues steadily with prescribing; a single spike usually follows a news story, a published study, or a law-firm advertising campaign.
Indication confounding
The reaction closely matches a condition the drug is prescribed for. Statins surface hyperlipidaemia and diabetes drugs surface blood glucose increased not because they cause these things, but because only people who already have them receive the drug.
Does not survive comparison
When recomputed against clinically similar drugs instead of the whole database, the ratio fell by 90% or more. Most of the apparent signal was the illness, the route of administration, or the reporting environment — not the molecule. Cerivastatin and motor neurone disease falls from 394 to 2.2 this way.
